# AI Assistance Log

AI tools, primarily ChatGPT, were used during this project as a development and research assistant.

AI assistance included:

- Brainstorming and refining the frame-selection research direction.
- Reviewing and suggesting Python implementation and testing approaches.
- Helping debug OpenGait integration, dataset processing, and experiment scripts.
- Discussing experimental controls, corruption protocols, and result interpretation.
- Assisting with organization and wording for documentation and presentation material.

All commands and experiments were executed by the project author. Experimental outputs, dataset checks, test results, and reported numerical results were reviewed and verified before being included in the project.
